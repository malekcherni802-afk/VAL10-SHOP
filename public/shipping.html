VAL10 · STORE POLICY

Shipping
Information.

VAL10 applies a flat delivery fee of 8.00 TND per order within Tunisia. The delivery fee is shown separately at checkout and included in the cash-on-delivery total before you confirm your order.

Order processing

After submitting an order, keep your phone available so the store can verify the selected item, delivery address and delivery arrangements. Orders are prepared after confirmation.

Delivery and payment

Payment is collected on delivery (cash on delivery). The checkout total includes the product price plus the 8.00 TND delivery fee. If a destination is outside the available delivery area, VAL10 will contact the customer before dispatch.

Delays or address changes

Delivery timing may vary depending on the destination and courier availability. Contact VAL10 promptly through its official customer contact channel if your address or availability changes.

Delivery fee: 8.00 TND per order in Tunisia. Delivery timing and coverage are confirmed by phone after the order is received.
