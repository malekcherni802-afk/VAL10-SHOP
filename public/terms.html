VAL10 · STORE POLICY

Terms of
Purchase.

By submitting an order through the VAL10 website, you confirm that the contact and delivery details you provide are accurate and that you are requesting the listed product and selected variants.

Orders and confirmation

Submitting the online form creates an order request and reserves the selected stock in the store system while the order is pending confirmation or cancellation. VAL10 may contact you by telephone to confirm the order and arrange delivery. An order is not considered finally accepted until the store confirms it.

Products and pricing

Prices are displayed in Tunisian dinars (TND). Product photographs are provided to represent each item; minor differences in colour may occur between screens and the physical product. Product availability is subject to stock at the time the order is submitted.

Payment and delivery

The current storefront indicates cash on delivery. Delivery arrangements and the applicable delivery details are confirmed with the customer by the store before dispatch. See the Shipping Policy for the current store information.

Order changes

For a correction or cancellation, contact VAL10 as soon as possible using the official customer contact channel and provide your order reference. A request may not be possible to change after dispatch.
