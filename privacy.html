VAL10 · STORE POLICY

Privacy
Policy.

VAL10 uses the information submitted through the order form to receive, confirm and deliver customer orders.

Information we collect

When you place an order, the store receives your name, telephone number, delivery address and the product, size and colour selected. The site may also process basic technical information needed to operate the service securely.

How information is used

Order information is used to contact you about your order, confirm availability, arrange delivery and respond to order-related questions. The current checkout indicates cash on delivery; the website does not ask for payment-card details.

Access and sharing

Order information is available to authorised VAL10 store administrators and the hosting or database providers needed to operate the website. Information may be shared with a delivery provider when necessary to complete a delivery, or where disclosure is required by law. VAL10 does not offer customer order details for sale.

Storage and requests

Order records are retained for as long as reasonably needed to manage orders, customer service and applicable business or legal records. To ask about an order record or request a correction, contact VAL10 through its official customer contact channel.

Policy review: the store owner should confirm the legal business identity, retention period, hosting providers and customer contact channel before launch. This page is an operational privacy summary, not legal advice.
